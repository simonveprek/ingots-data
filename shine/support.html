The space didn't come back after cleaning

Deleted photos stay in the Recently Deleted album for 30 days and keep using space until then. To free it right away: Photos → Albums → Recently Deleted → Select → Delete All.

Shine shows a different number than iPhone Storage

Shine counts only what it can clean: similar photos (the best shot of each series stays), screenshots, large videos and blurry photos. It also separates space on your iPhone from space in iCloud.

How do I keep a photo Shine wants to delete?

Tap a category on the home screen and tap the photo. Kept items are never deleted. Settings → Kept items shows how many you have.

Restore Shine Pro on a new iPhone

Settings → Restore Purchase. Pro is a one-time purchase and works with Family Sharing.

Po úklidu se místo neuvolnilo

Smazané fotky jsou 30 dní v albu Naposledy smazané a do té doby zabírají místo. Uvolníš ho hned: Fotky → Alba → Naposledy smazané → Vybrat → Smazat vše.

Jak fotku zachránit

Na domovské obrazovce klepni na kategorii a pak na fotku. Zachráněné se nikdy nesmažou.

Obnovení Pro na novém iPhonu

Nastavení → Obnovit nákup. Pro je jednorázový nákup s Rodinným sdílením.
