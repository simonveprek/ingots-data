FAQ

Where are my purchases stored?
Only on your iPhone. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.

How do I restore Ingots Pro on a new device?
Open Settings → Ingots Pro → Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account.

How do I cancel the monthly subscription?
iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Ingots.

Why is my portfolio value different from what a dealer pays?
Ingots values holdings at the spot price. Dealers usually buy back a few percent below spot.

Are forecasts investment advice?
No. They are statistical models that extrapolate past prices and cannot predict shocks.
