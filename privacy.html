Privacy Policy

Ingots · Last updated 6 November 2026

In short

Ingots does not collect, store or share any personal data. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

Data you enter

Your purchases (metal, weight, price, date, dealer, notes) and settings are stored only on your device, inside the app and its widgets. They are never sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Network requests

To show prices, the app downloads public market data from these services. The requests contain no personal information – only which commodity or currency is requested:

  • gold-api.com – live spot prices
  • GitHub (raw.githubusercontent.com, simonveprek.github.io) – price history collected for Ingots
  • World Bank – long-term monthly prices (CC BY 4.0)
  • Frankfurter / European Central Bank – exchange rates

Like any web server, these services may see your IP address when the app connects to them; we have no access to it.

Purchases

Ingots Pro is sold through Apple's App Store. Payment is handled entirely by Apple; we never see your payment details or Apple ID.

Children

Ingots is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

Stručně

Ingots nesbírá, neukládá ani nesdílí žádné osobní údaje. Žádný účet, žádná analytika, reklamy ani sledování.

Data, která zadáš

Nákupy (kov, hmotnost, cena, datum, prodejce, poznámky) a nastavení jsou uložené jen ve tvém zařízení – v aplikaci a jejích widgetech. Nikam se neodesílají. Smazáním aplikace se smažou i tato data.

Síťové požadavky

Kvůli cenám aplikace stahuje veřejná tržní data z gold-api.com, GitHubu (historie cen pro Ingots), Světové banky (CC BY 4.0) a Frankfurter/ECB (kurzy). Požadavky neobsahují žádné osobní údaje.
